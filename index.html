The Heritage & Legend

An Italian Sanctuary Carved Into Living Cliffside

Dating back to the 18th century as the coastal retreat of Neapolitan nobility, The Grand Horizon has been meticulously restored into a modern architectural masterpiece.

Each hand-quarried travertine stone, ancient olive garden, and soaring glass terrace was curated to honor the timeless poetry of the Amalfi Coast while cocooning discerning travelers in contemporary ultra-luxury, intuitive privacy, and bespoke butler pampering.
